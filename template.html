How the ranking works

The list is ordered by each player's average TTFL score over his last 5 games. The smaller "proj" number is tonight's projection, explained below.

TTFL score = points + rebounds + assists + steals + blocks + made shots + made threes + made free throws − turnovers − missed shots − missed threes − missed free throws. So a made three is worth +5 (3 points, +1 shot, +1 three) and a missed three −2.

Form blends the season average (half), the last 10 games (30%) and the last 5 (20%). Early in a season the current season is mixed with last season, moving fully to the current season after 15 games.

Matchup uses the betting line when there is one: the game total and spread give each team's expected points, compared to the league average. Without a line it uses the teams' scoring and points allowed. Back-to-back −5%. Home +1.5%, away −1.5%. Blowout risk (spread of 10 or more) trims starters by 5%. Teammates out adds up to 10% for rotation players when regulars on their team are ruled out. Each factor is shown as the TTFL points it adds or removes from the player's form.

Expected = projection × chance of playing (out 0%, doubtful 20%, questionable or day-to-day 55%, probable 95%). Data from ESPN: schedule, odds, rosters, injury report and game logs.
